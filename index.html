Frequently Asked Questions

What thickness vapor barrier is recommended for a crawlspace?
Building science experts recommend at least a 10 mil to 12 mil virgin polyethylene reinforced barrier. Cheap 6 mil black plastic deteriorates within 3 to 5 years, tears easily when crawled upon, and emits odors. Premium full encapsulation jobs frequently use 20 mil reinforced barriers.
How wide should vapor barrier seams overlap?
All seams between adjacent rolls must overlap by at least 12 inches (1 foot) and be continuously taped with heavy-duty 4-inch waterproof white seam tape. Never use standard duct tape, which loses adhesion in damp conditions within weeks.
Should a crawlspace vapor barrier go up the foundation walls?
Yes. Modern building codes (IRC Section R408.3) require vapor barriers to run at least 6 to 12 inches up exterior masonry walls (fastened with acoustic sealant and masonry pins), leaving a 3-inch gap below the sill plate for mandatory termite inspection.
